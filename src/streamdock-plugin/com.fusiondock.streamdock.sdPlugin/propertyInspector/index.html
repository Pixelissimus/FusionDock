Not a setting — it follows the action you placed. Use Fusion Key (Landscape) for 5 wide by 3 tall, dial bottom right, or Fusion Key (Portrait) for 3 wide by 5 tall, dial top right. Whichever you placed on most keys wins.

To switch, replace the keys with the other action. There is no dropdown on purpose: the device always reports the same size however it is held, so a setting here could disagree with how you are actually holding it — and then every key lands in the wrong place.

Icon rotation is separate and belongs to VSD Craft's own per-device rotation setting.
